Don't Lose Your Head · Cinemacity Halloween campaign

Privacy Notice

Effective date · October 2026

1. Introduction

Cinemacity respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Notice explains how information is collected, used, stored and protected when you participate in the Don't Lose Your Head Halloween game and prize draw.

2. Information We Collect

When you register for the prize draw, we may collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • UAE mobile number
  • Preferred Cinemacity location
  • Preferred screening date
  • Instagram username, if voluntarily provided
  • Marketing communication preferences

We may also process gameplay information, entry status, technical information and basic interaction data where these features are enabled.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information may be used to:

  • Register and validate prize draw entries.
  • Administer the competition and select eligible winners.
  • Contact winners and coordinate prize collection.
  • Prevent duplicate entries, fraud and misuse.
  • Respond to participant questions.
  • Improve the technical performance of the game.
  • Send Cinemacity news, screening updates and promotional offers, only where you have separately opted in.

Participation in the prize draw does not require consent to receive marketing communications.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process personal information as necessary to administer the promotion, fulfil our obligations to participants, comply with applicable legal requirements and protect the integrity of the campaign.

Where consent is required, including for optional direct marketing, we will request it separately.

5. Data Storage and Retention

Personal information will be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to administer the competition, distribute prizes, resolve disputes and meet applicable legal obligations.

Prize draw data will ordinarily be deleted or anonymised within 90 days after the campaign ends, unless longer retention is legally required.

Information used for optional marketing will be retained until consent is withdrawn or another applicable retention requirement applies.

6. Sharing Your Information

Cinemacity may share relevant information with authorised service providers, technical partners and campaign agencies involved in operating the game and administering the prize draw.

Such parties may process information only for authorised purposes and subject to appropriate confidentiality and data protection safeguards.

We do not sell participant personal information.

7. Local Storage and Cookies

The game may use browser local storage to maintain gameplay information, record participation status and support game functionality.

Additional cookies or analytics technologies may be used if enabled. Where legally required, appropriate notice and consent will be obtained.

8. International Data Transfers

If personal information is processed or stored outside the UAE, Cinemacity will apply appropriate safeguards as required by applicable data protection laws.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request to:

  • Access personal information held about you.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of eligible personal information.
  • Withdraw marketing consent.
  • Object to or request restrictions on certain processing activities.

Requests can be submitted through Cinemacity's official contact channels.

10. Marketing Communications

Receiving promotional communications is optional.

Participants who choose to receive marketing may withdraw their consent at any time using the unsubscribe instructions provided or by contacting Cinemacity.

Withdrawing marketing consent does not affect an otherwise valid prize draw entry.

11. Data Security

Cinemacity will use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure.

No online system can guarantee absolute security.

12. Updates to This Notice

Cinemacity may update this Privacy Notice to reflect operational, legal or technical changes.

Any updated version will be published through the official campaign website.
